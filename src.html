Is my progress saved?

Yes — this browser remembers which round your ant is on and which rooms you have mopped. Clearing your browser data, or opening the game in a private window, starts you over.

Can I play with a friend?

Open a room in either game and send them the code. The same code works in both — the ants you sort with can pick up mops with you.

Do I need an account?

No. Nothing is stored about you beyond the name you choose to type.
